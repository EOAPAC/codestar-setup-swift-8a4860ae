Design the homepage hero section for a minimal, editorial awards brand. The section is anchored by a large crystal award trophy placed on the right side of the viewport, with the headline and call-to-action stacked on the left. Use a full-width, light background section with a centered content container and generous horizontal padding. Place the trophy image as a large, absolute-positioned element flush to the right edge of the section, spanning the full section height, and sitting behind the text; hide it on mobile and tablet, and show it only on desktop. Left-align the text block inside the container and keep it narrow enough to stay clear of the trophy, using a subtle horizontal gradient overlay over the trophy image so the text remains legible: from solid background color at the left through a semi-transparent background to transparent at the right, leaving the trophy crisp and visible on the right third of the viewport. Set the typography in Inter, sans-serif, with a primary brand blue of #1978E5. Include a small rounded eyebrow pill with a thin border, secondary background, and a tiny blue dot, in xs sentence-case text with a muted grey tone. The headline should be very large, semibold, and tightly tracked, with the final clause in primary blue, for example: "For the person who built it." Use a subhead in body grey with relaxed leading, large enough to read comfortably but clearly subordinate. The trophy should be a tall, clear crystal or glass award photographed on a clean white or transparent background, with a subtle etched seal on the front such as "Entrepreneur Awards Winner 2026," and it should not be cropped, framed, shadowed, or tinted. Treat the image as decorative and hide it from screen readers. Add two call-to-action buttons: a filled blue primary button labeled "Enter now →" and a ghost secondary button labeled "See how it works," arranged horizontally on desktop and stacked vertically on mobile. At mobile, the trophy should be hidden, text should be full width, and spacing should be reduced; at tablet, keep the trophy hidden and increase spacing; at desktop, show the trophy, place the text in the left half, and set the buttons side by side. The overall mood should be calm, credible, editorial, and warm, with no gradients on buttons, no shadows on the trophy image, and no gold or luxury styling. The trophy should feel like a clear piece of evidence rather than a decorative prize.
